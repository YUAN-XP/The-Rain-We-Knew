## About / 游戏介绍

I am developing a 2D narrative exploration and puzzle game about memory and companionship. A camera and an oil lamp connect different spaces and states of the world; returning through familiar places reveals new clues and changes their meaning.

这是我制作的一款围绕记忆与陪伴展开的二维叙事探索解谜游戏。相机与油灯连接不同空间与世界状态，通过场景节点回环，让玩家在重访熟悉地点时发现新的线索。

## Progress and next steps / 当前进度与后续

The current demo includes a playable main story from the opening through the ending, exploration and puzzles, minigames, inventory, saves, music, and Chinese/English language switching. The main route has been tested; this is still an early preview, not the finished game.

目前已接通从开场到结尾的 Demo 主线，以及探索解谜、小游戏、背包、存档、配乐和中英切换，并完成主线测试。**所有美术、人物、动画和音乐仍需进一步人工打磨，现有生成素材与原型动画尚未达到我最终希望呈现的品质。** 后续将继续完善视觉与动作、声音、翻译、交互体验和兼容性，并进一步落实完整设计。

All art, characters, animation and music still need further human refinement. Generated assets and prototype animation do not yet represent my intended final quality. Visuals, motion, audio, translation, interaction and compatibility remain in development.
